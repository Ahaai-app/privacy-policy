2. How the information is used

  • To run the App — calculate your calorie and macro targets, save your logs, show your history and streaks, and sync across your devices.
  • To identify food — process photos, barcodes, and text descriptions to estimate nutrition values.
  • To personalise your experience — tailor goals, reminders, and suggestions to the profile you entered.
  • To manage subscriptions — unlock premium features and confirm your purchase status with Google Play.
  • To fix problems — diagnose crashes, reproduce bugs, and improve performance.
  • To improve the App — understand which features are used and which are ignored, using aggregated and de-identified data wherever possible.
  • To answer you — respond to support emails, bug reports, and privacy requests.
  • To keep the App safe and legal — prevent abuse and fraud, and comply with laws or valid legal requests.
What I do not do: I do not sell your personal information. I do not share your food logs, weight, photos, or health data with advertisers or data brokers. I do not use your health data to target ads to you.

3. AI food recognition

When you photograph a meal or describe it in words, that content is sent to a third-party AI provider so the nutrition can be estimated. The provider processes the image or text and returns a result to the App.

  • Photos are sent for the purpose of analysis only.
  • Estimates produced by AI are approximate. They can be wrong — especially for mixed dishes, home cooking, hidden oils, and unusual portion sizes. Please treat every number as an estimate, not a measurement.
  • The AI provider processes this data under its own terms as my service provider. The AI provider used by Calaro is OpenAI. See the OpenAI Privacy Policy.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the App: [Settings → Account → Delete account — update to match your real menu path]
  • By email: send a deletion request to admin@ahaai.com from the address linked to your account

Deletion removes your profile, food logs, weight history, and photos. Some records may be kept longer where the law requires it — for example, purchase records needed for tax or refund purposes. Anonymised and aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

Deleting the App from your phone does not delete your account data from the server. Use one of the two methods above.

11. Children

Calaro is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided information through the App, email me and I will investigate and delete the account and the associated data.

12. Health disclaimer

Calaro is a tracking and information tool, not a medical service. Calorie and macro figures are estimates and may be inaccurate. Nothing in the App is medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment plan.

Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, an athlete in training, or managing any medical condition.

I do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA, and I do not use your health or nutrition data for cross-context behavioural advertising. Saudi Arabia

As the App is operated from Saudi Arabia, processing is also carried out in line with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its implementing regulations. Saudi residents may contact me to access, correct, or request deletion of their personal data, and may raise concerns with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).
